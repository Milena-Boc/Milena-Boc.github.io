III Perspective

Questions I keep turning over

Open questions I'm still working through.

Experiences That Evolve

Access to AI is cheap. Fluency is not.

People in guest-facing roles are encouraged to use AI, often by their own companies, but rarely given the structure to become fluent in it. Having watched technology adoption across three countries and studied AI at Oxford, I'm exploring what it takes to make it practical, ethical and second nature.

Exploring What Comes Next

The gap may widen before it closes.

I think AI could push mid-scale hospitality in one of two directions: towards more refined, more profitable service, or further behind. The top of the market, already resourced to adopt it well, may pull further ahead.

Professionals Who Evolve

A career that zigzags is not a detour.

From the stage and musical theatre to weddings in Mexico, resorts in Maine, a hotel in Cambridge and now AI. What I have noticed is that range compounds: each move made the next one make sense. I'm exploring how young professionals can carry their individual passions into what comes next, and tell that story with confidence.

Personal app

Fitness and nutrition, built for one

I built this because I kept needing guidance on exactly what to do. I already knew the training I love, mostly CrossFit, so I wanted to remove the mental load of choosing a session every morning.

Having my macros to hand on the go helped just as much, instead of digging through my notes or asking Google every time.
