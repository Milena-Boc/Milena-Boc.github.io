I

StoryBuilt across three countries

It all started in Mexico: a boutique wedding and events business, built for international couples marrying somewhere they'd fallen in love with, and wanting to bring the people who matter to them to experience the warmth of Mexico.

Maine came next: two luxury resort properties, front desk to restaurant management to running events. That cross-training teaches you, in detail, that a hotel is one enormous machine with many rooms, each with its own design and its own customer journey:

  • Some for guests
  • Some for food and drink
  • Some for the events themselves

Alongside it, a bilingual community I founded to connect English and Spanish speakers across hospitality, business, and later technology. Three years of building something bilingual, because I've lived what it means to work between two languages myself.

Cambridge is where it is now: rebuilding the events department of a Hilton property into a commercial engine, built on data and innovation.

In between all of it, a formal detour into Oxford's Saïd Business School AI Programme. Not a pivot away from hospitality, but the other half of a question I ask myself in everything I do: how do you deliver something that holds value, something memorable and genuinely excellent, for the people running it and the people receiving it.

The throughline is ownership: start with a vision, then work backwards toward it, one deliberate step at a time. Build in the feedback loops early. Stay alert to the routes you hadn't planned on. Connect the dots backwards until they resolve into something real, a project, a vision, an event.
